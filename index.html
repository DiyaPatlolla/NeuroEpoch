CENTRAL REGION (LEFT HEMISPHERE)

Electrode Site: C3

Located over the left sensorimotor cortex (Rolandic area). Essential in polysomnography and clinical EEG for registering vertex sharp waves, sleep spindles (11–16 Hz), and mu rhythm. Referenced to contralateral mastoid (M2) in standard AASM PSG montage.

⚡ Clinical Electrophysiology: Phase Reversal Localization

In a bipolar longitudinal ("Double Banana") montage, when an epileptic spike occurs under electrode C3:

• Channel 1 (F3 - C3): Grid 2 is more negative → Deflects DOWNWARD
• Channel 2 (C3 - P3): Grid 1 is more negative → Deflects UPWARD
• Result: The two channels point toward each other, localizing the focus to C3!

📖 AASM Scoring Manual Reference (v2.6 / v3.0 Criteria)

Stage W (Wakefulness)

Scored when > 50% of the epoch has Posterior Dominant Rhythm (Alpha, 8–13 Hz) in occipital derivations (O1-M2, O2-M1) with eyes closed. Also characterized by eye blinks (0.5–2 Hz), rapid voluntary eye movements, and high tonic submental EMG.

Stage N1 (NREM 1 - Drowsiness / Sleep Onset)

Scored when alpha rhythm attenuates and is replaced by Low-Amplitude Mixed-Frequency (LAMF, 4–7 Hz Theta) activity for > 50% of the epoch. Accompanied by Slow Rolling Eye Movements (SEMs) on EOG and prominent Vertex Sharp Waves (< 200 ms duration) in central channels.

Stage N2 (NREM 2 - Core Light Sleep)

Scored when either of the following occurs during the epoch or preceding epoch (without arousal):
1. One or more Sleep Spindles: Distinct burst of 11–16 Hz (usually 12–14 Hz) sinusoidal waves lasting ≥ 0.5 seconds (maximal central).
2. One or more K-Complexes: Well-delineated sharp negative wave followed by positive wave lasting ≥ 0.5 seconds (maximal frontal).

Stage N3 (NREM 3 - Slow Wave Deep Sleep)

Scored when ≥ 20% of the 30-second epoch (≥ 6 seconds) consists of slow wave activity: delta waves with frequency 0.5–2.0 Hz and peak-to-peak amplitude > 75 μV (measured in frontal derivations F4-M1 or F3-M2).

Stage R (REM - Rapid Eye Movement Sleep)

Scored when all three criteria are met simultaneously:
1. Low-amplitude mixed-frequency EEG (desynchronized, may exhibit 2–6 Hz Sawtooth Waves).
2. Submental EMG tone reaches baseline Atonia (lowest tone of the entire recording).
3. Bursts of Rapid Eye Movements (REMs) on EOG channels.
